Keybinds Unlocked

The keybinds macOS won't give you.

Double-tap ⌘ for Mission Control. Mouse side button for Show Desktop.

Free. macOS 13 or later. Not signed by Apple, so the first time you open it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
